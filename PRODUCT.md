# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Adult creators share a public profile with supporters who want to send a gift, fund a goal, or leave a private note. The first surface is the creator profile, designed to turn attention into a clear, private action.

## Product Purpose

Defant gives creators one intimate, controlled place for wishes, goals, and fan support without exposing personal details. The MVP should make a supporter understand what to do in seconds and make a creator feel represented rather than processed.

## Brand Personality

Confident, intimate, precise. The voice is direct and adult without being explicit. It should feel like a well-made nightlife publication, not an adult marketplace or a generic SaaS dashboard.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic creator economy gradients, pastel donation widgets, excessive rounded cards, emoji-led UI, fake luxury, and AI-generated “sexy” visuals. Do not copy Throne, Wishlistr, or Ko-fi; borrow their clarity around lists, privacy, and direct support.

## Design Principles

1. Desire should be easy to act on.
2. Privacy is part of the experience, not a legal footnote.
3. The creator's taste leads the page.
4. A small number of strong choices beats a wall of features.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for text and controls. Support keyboard focus, reduced motion, clear labels, and responsive layouts. The product is for adults and must keep the interface sensual without relying on explicit imagery.
