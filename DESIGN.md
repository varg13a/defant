# Defant design system

## Direction

Dark nightlife editorial: charcoal plum surfaces, one restrained wine accent, DM Sans for utility, Playfair Display for the creator's voice. Imagery is photographic and tactile; decoration is sparse.

## Tokens

- Background: `#171317`
- Surface: `#211a20`
- Ink: `#f3ece8`
- Muted: `#b4a8a7`
- Accent: `#c46a78`
- Line: `rgba(243,236,232,.18)`

## Layout

The hero uses an editorial split with copy on the left and a portrait on the right. Wishlist content uses one large feature and two supporting wishes rather than identical cards. Mobile collapses to one column with the portrait after the intro.

## Interaction

Actions use tactile color change and small vertical lift. Reduced motion disables movement. Gift and message actions currently surface a local toast as MVP behavior.
